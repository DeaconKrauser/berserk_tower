---
name: bastiao-gamefeel
description: Game feel and combat feedback rules for Bastiao. Use for tower attacks, enemy damage, commander powers, bosses, building and upgrades.
---

Every important gameplay action should have readable feedback.

Tower attack:
- anticipation where appropriate
- projectile/cast
- impact effect
- sound

Enemy damage:
- very short hit flash
- hit sound
- restrained reaction
- optional damage indicator

Heavy attacks:
- stronger impact sound
- short camera impulse
- larger effect

Boss arrival:
- music transition
- boss health bar
- short visual emphasis
- boss intro animation

Building:
- placement sound
- brief construction effect
- visual confirmation

Upgrade:
- sound
- visual pulse/effect
- visible model/sprite change where available

Do not overuse:
- screen shake
- flashes
- chromatic effects
- large damage numbers

Feedback must improve readability, not obscure gameplay.
